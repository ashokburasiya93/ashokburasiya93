## Featured projects
### [BenefitBridge](https://github.com/ashokburasiya93/benefitbridge)
Many households qualify for public support after a life event but never claim it. BenefitBridge finds them and hands a caseworker a pre-filled application and a draft message to approve.
- AI reads letters, notes and messages in any language and must quote its source for every fact; plain-code rules decide eligibility and cite the clause; a caseworker approves every message
- North Star: eligible households enrolled within 30 days, from 21% today to 45% in the pilot and 60% at rollout, against $160,770 a year left unclaimed
- Live evals: 98.7% fact recall, 100% eligibility precision and recall, zero safety failures; about $132 a month on Groq gpt-oss-120b with Claude as backup
- Full AI PM package: user research, AI opportunity map, PRD with North Star metric, cost model, Next.js prototype, deck

### [CloudBill Detective](https://github.com/ashokburasiya93/cloudbill-detective)
Cost tools tell you the cloud bill went up. CloudBill Detective tells you which change caused it, who owns it, and the fix to approve.
- Joins AWS billing line items with the change log (deploys, Terraform, CloudTrail) to find the cause of every sustained cost change, with evidence and ruled-out red herrings
- Splits usage from price, routes each case to an owner, drafts a fix for human approval; read-only by design
- Every number in AI-written text is fact-checked; 12 launch evals run as a build gate
- Full AI PM package: user research, AI opportunity map, PRD with North Star metric, cost model, deck
